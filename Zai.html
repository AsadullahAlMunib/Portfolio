About Me

I'm Md Asadullah Al Munib from Bangladesh, a student with a deep interest in technology and creativity. I grew up with a natural curiosity about how things work — which led me to explore coding, robotics, and DIY projects alongside my studies. I passed my Dakhil (SSC) in 2025 from Dhap Satgara B. M. Model Kamil Madrasah, Rangpur, and I'm currently a student of the Alim-2027 (HSC-27) batch at the same institution.

I'm learning and practicing web design & development — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, and Laravel — plus C programming. Outside of studies, I enjoy writing short articles on science and technology, and I serve as an Ambassador of ICT Olympiad Bangladesh. I believe combining academic knowledge with self-learning opens the door to real growth.

My goal is to continuously improve my skills, create meaningful projects, and inspire people through innovation and passion.
